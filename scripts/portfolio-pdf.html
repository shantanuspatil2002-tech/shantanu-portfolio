How I work

The same five moves, every time

Shown on the Prodmax engagement, the cleanest end-to-end example.

01 Diagnose
Look before touching anything
Process mapping, capacity analysis and shopfloor observation to find the real bottleneck.
02 Structure
Turn it into a model
A linear-programming optimiser in Excel Solver across 12 SKUs.
03 Decide
