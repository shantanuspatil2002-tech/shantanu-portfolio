05 · Case study · Operations consulting, delivered

Optimising manufacturing performance at Prodmax Global

Independent Operations Consultant (pro bono) · Oct 2023 – Aug 2025

ProblemA manufacturing SME operating below capacity, with no formal process discipline and competing priorities across capacity, lead time and profitability.
DiagnosisProcess mapping, capacity analysis and shopfloor observation to find bottlenecks and operational constraints before redesigning any workflow.
DecisionBuilt a linear-programming optimiser (Excel Solver) across 12 SKUs and shifted scheduling from volume-based to margin-based, sequencing implementation by the trade-offs across capacity, lead time and profitability. A lean pass (5S, Kanban) followed in Phase 2, with 20+ operators and supervisors trained to sustain it.
ImpactMonthly profit +14.4% (₹4.5L → ₹5.15L) and utilisation 70% → 91% in 12 weeks. Lead time -17%, material waste -13.4%, quarterly revenue +7.12%. CEO-verified in writing.
